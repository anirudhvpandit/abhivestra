Known modelling boundaries

Read before using the outputs for a business decision.

  • Monthly volume changes compound; fractional units are permitted.
  • Cash collection and operating payment factors apply within each month. No receivables or payables balance is rolled forward.
  • Tax is estimated on positive pre-tax income; tax timing and jurisdiction-specific rules are not modeled.
  • Scenario and capital-investment outputs are illustrative and are not accounting, tax, investment, or legal advice.
  • This local app has no user authentication, organization membership, or multi-tenant access control.
